I'm a PhD student in Engineering Mathematics at the University of Bristol, funded by a University of Bristol Scholarship / BRACE Studentship, supervised by Dr. Qiang Liu and Prof. Liz Coulthard. My research develops interpretable AI models that combine wearable sleep data, genetics, and clinical variables to predict Alzheimer's disease risk before significant cognitive decline occurs, using UK Biobank data.

My background is in neuroscience (MSci) and health data science (MSc), with experience in statistical modelling, time-series analysis, and classification pipelines from prior work including gait analysis and neuroimaging.

This site is where I'll be logging my PhD journey — what I'm learning, building, and thinking about as the research develops. Expect posts on survival analysis, deep learning for time-series, explainable AI, and the occasional reflection on academic life.

### Interests
- Multimodal AI for healthcare
- Explainable/interpretable machine learning
- Sleep, neurodegeneration, and neurological injury
- Survival analysis and time-series modelling
