Today marks the start of my PhD in Engineering Mathematics at the University of Bristol, working on multimodal AI for early detection and risk prediction of Alzheimer's disease.

My plan for the first two quarters is to build technical foundations — survival analysis (Cox models, Kaplan-Meier), time-series deep learning, and explainable AI methods — while getting familiar with UK Biobank data access. I'll be posting updates here as I go: what I'm learning, what's working, what isn't, and where the research is heading.
